Example 2.7.8. Example IV.

Calculate the triple integral below by converting it to spherical coordinates:
🔗
\begin{equation*} \int_{-2}^{2}\!\!\int_{-\sqrt{4-x^2}}^{\sqrt{4-x^2}} \!\!\int_{2-\sqrt{4-x^2-y^2}}^{2+\sqrt{4-x^2-y^2}} \left(x^2+y^2+z^2\right)^{3/2} dz\,dy\,dx. \end{equation*}
Solution. (Read the solid off the limits: the \(z\)-limits are the two halves of a sphere; which one, and where is its center? It is shown in Figure 2.7.9. Convert its equation to spherical coordinates as in Example 2.7.2, and describe the solid by the ranges of \(\theta\text{,}\) \(\phi\) and \(\rho\text{;}\) the integrand is a power of \(\rho\text{.}\) Then apply (2.7.3); the \(\phi\)-integral is a substitution.)
