Example 3.2.8. Both Sides of Stokes’ Theorem.

Verify (3.2.2) for the field \(\mathbf{F} = -y\,\mathbf{i} + x\,\mathbf{j}\) and the upper hemisphere \(S\) of \(x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1\text{,}\) oriented by its upward unit normal.
🔗
Solution. (The boundary of \(S\) is the unit circle in the \(xy\)-plane; use the right-hand rule to decide its direction, parametrize it, and compute the circulation. For the other side, compute \(\nabla\times\mathbf F\) by (3.2.1); on the hemisphere the outward unit normal is (2.10.4) and, by (2.10.3), \(d\sigma = \sin\phi\,d\phi\,d\theta\text{.}\) Integrate over the upper hemisphere and compare. The surface is shown in Figure 3.2.9.)
