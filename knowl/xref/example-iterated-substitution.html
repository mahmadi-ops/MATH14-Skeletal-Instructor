Example 2.1.9. Example II.

Calculate the double integral \(\displaystyle\int_0^2\!\!\int_0^1 \frac{x}{1+xy}\,dx\,dy\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution. (By Theorem 2.1.8 you may integrate over \(y\) first, with \(x\) held constant; the substitution \(u = 1 + xy\) does it. The result is a function of \(x\) involving a logarithm, which needs integration by parts, \(\int u\,dv = uv - \int v\,du\text{;}\) in the leftover integral, write the numerator as \((1 + 2x) - 1\text{.}\) The surface is shown in Figure 2.1.10.)
