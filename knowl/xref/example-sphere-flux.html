Example 2.10.11. Flux Across the Unit Sphere.

Find the flux of the vector field \(\mathbf{F}(x,y,z) = z\,\mathbf{i} + y\,\mathbf{j} + x\,\mathbf{k}\) across the unit sphere \(x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution. (Use the spherical parametrization (2.10.1) with its parameter ranges, so that (2.10.6) applies; the cross product is (2.10.3), and check that it points outward. Substitute the parametrization into \(\mathbf{F}\text{,}\) dot, and integrate over the parameter rectangle; one of the two terms integrates to zero over a full period of \(\theta\text{.}\) Compare with Figure 2.10.12.)
