Example 3.2.20. Trading a Paraboloid for a Disk.

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\iint_S (\nabla \times \mathbf{F}) \cdot \hat{\mathbf{n}}\, d\sigma\) for \(\mathbf{F} = y\,\mathbf{i} + z\,\mathbf{j} + x\,\mathbf{k}\text{,}\) where \(S\) is the part of the paraboloid \(z = 4 - x^2 - y^2\) above the \(xy\)-plane, oriented upward.
🔗
Solution. (Find the boundary of \(S\) and the direction the upward orientation gives it. By (3.2.3) you may replace the paraboloid by any surface with the same boundary and compatible orientation: the flat disk of Figure 3.2.21 is the easy one. Compute the curl, dot with the disk’s normal, and integrate. As a check, parametrize the boundary circle and compute the circulation directly.)
