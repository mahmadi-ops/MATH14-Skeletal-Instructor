Remark 3.2.5.

Green’s theorem is the flat case. If \(S\) is a region \(R\) of the \(xy\)-plane with \(\hat{\mathbf{n}} = \mathbf{k}\text{,}\) then \(d\sigma = dx\,dy\) and \((\nabla \times \mathbf{F}) \cdot \mathbf{k}\) is the \(\mathbf{k}\)-component of the curl, so (3.2.2) becomes (3.1.5) word for word.
