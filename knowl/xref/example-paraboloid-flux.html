Example 2.10.13. Flux Across a Closed Surface.

Evaluate the surface integral \(\displaystyle \iint_S \mathbf{F} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{n}}\, d\sigma\text{,}\) where \(\mathbf{F}(x,y,z) = y\,\mathbf{i} + x\,\mathbf{j} + z\,\mathbf{k}\) and the surface \(S\) is the boundary of the solid region \(E\) enclosed by the paraboloid \(z = 1 - x^2 - y^2\) and the plane \(z = 0\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution. (The closed surface has two pieces, shown in Figure 2.10.15: the paraboloid cap \(S_1\) and the unit disk \(S_2\) in the plane \(z = 0\text{.}\) Which way must the outward normal point on each? Parametrize the cap as a graph, \(\mathbf{r}(x,y) = \langle x, y, 1 - x^2 - y^2\rangle\text{,}\) and compute \(\mathbf{r}_x \times \mathbf{r}_y\text{;}\) check its \(z\)-component against the required orientation. Do the same for the disk, fixing the sign if necessary. Then apply (2.10.6) to each piece over the unit disk \(R\text{;}\) on the cap, one term vanishes by symmetry and polar coordinates finish the rest. Compare with Figure 2.10.14.)
