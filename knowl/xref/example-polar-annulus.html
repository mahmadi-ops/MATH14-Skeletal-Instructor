Example 2.4.3. Example I.

Evaluate \(\displaystyle\iint_R (3x + 4y^2)\,dA\text{,}\) where \(R\) is the region in the upper half plane bounded by the circles \(x^2 + y^2 = 1\) and \(x^2 + y^2 = 4\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution. (Sketch the region, shown in Figure 2.4.4, and describe it in polar coordinates. Convert the integrand with (2.3.1), remember the factor \(r\) in \(dA\text{,}\) integrate in \(r\) first, and use the half-angle identity for \(\sin^2\theta\) in the outer integral.)
